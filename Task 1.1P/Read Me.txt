1. Short description of my system

The system represents the porch and hallway lights of Linda's house.
A push button is used as the input.
When the button is pressed, both LEDs turn on.
Porch LED remains on for 30 seconds.
Hallway LED remains on for 60 seconds.
Pins:
Porch LED → D11
Hallway LED → D10
Button → D9
INPUT_PULLUP is used for the button.
The Arduino controls the LEDs through digital output pins.

The porch light stays on for 30 seconds and the hallway light for 60 seconds.


2. Explanation of code 

setup():
two LED pins as OUTPUT
button pin as INPUT_PULLUP
both LEDs initially OFF

loop():
Arduino continuously checks whether the button has been pressed. Because INPUT_PULLUP is used, the button press is detected when the input reads LOW.

Light-control section
Turn both LEDs on when the button is pressed, then:

waits 30 seconds and turns the porch LED off;
waits another 30 seconds and turns the hallway LED off.

This gives the hallway a total ON time of 60 seconds.
